# Dictionary Attack

A dictionary attack is a method in which words and phrases from a previously compiled "dictionary" are tried systematically in order to guess a password. Since many users often use common words or simple combinations, such an attack can often succeed faster than a pure brute-force attack, which tries all possible character combinations.

This method is based on the assumption that the password sought is contained in the list – which is why dictionaries with common passwords, names or frequently used terms are often used.

Individually created (personalised) dictionaries based on already known passwords and their variations can considerably increase the effectiveness of dictionary attacks.
