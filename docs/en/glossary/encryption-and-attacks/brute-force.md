# Brute-Force Attack

A brute-force attack (also called "exhaustive search") is a method in which all possible combinations (e.g. of passwords or keys) are tried systematically until the correct solution is found. This method often requires enormous computing power and time, especially if the length and complexity of the key sought are high.
