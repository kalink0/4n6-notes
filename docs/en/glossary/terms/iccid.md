# ICCID

The ICCID (Integrated Circuit Card Identifier) is the unique serial number of a SIM card or an eSIM profile. It typically consists of 19–20 digits and is structured according to the international standard ITU-T E.118. The ICCID serves to identify the SIM card, not the device or the mobile phone contract.

Structure:

- **MII (Major Industry Identifier):** identifies the industry (e.g. 89 = telecommunications)
- **Country code:** e.g. 49 for Germany
- **Issuer identifier:** identifies the mobile network operator
- **Individual serial number** of the SIM
- **Check digit** (Luhn checksum)

An ICCID is assigned to exactly one SIM card or one eSIM profile.
