# SIM card

A SIM card (Subscriber Identity Module) is a smart card that identifies and authenticates a mobile subscriber to the mobile network.

Among other things, the IMSI, the ICCID and a secret key for registering in the network are stored on the SIM card; the key cannot be read out. Common form factors are mini, micro and nano SIM. The SIM card is usually protected by a PIN: after three wrong entries it is blocked and can only be unblocked with the PUK; after ten wrong PUK entries it is permanently unusable.

From a digital forensics perspective, besides the IMSI and ICCID, the SIM card can store, for example, contacts, SMS, last dialled numbers and the last used Location Area Code (LAC). Current smartphones usually store contacts, messages and call logs on the device rather than on the SIM card.
