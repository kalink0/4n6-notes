# Time Zones

Time zones are geographically defined regions in which the same legal time applies.  
They are mostly based on longitude and are oriented towards Coordinated Universal Time (UTC).

IT systems, e.g. a notebook or mobile phone, can store timestamps relative to different time zones. When interpreting timestamps, it is important to take the underlying time zone into account. Which time zone an IT system uses depends on its time zone setting.

In Germany, the time zone "Central European Time" (UTC+0100) applies. Summer time is "Central European Summer Time" (UTC+0200).
